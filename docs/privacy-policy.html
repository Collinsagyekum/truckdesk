TruckDesk

Privacy Policy

Effective September 13, 2026

TruckDesk is operated by Numda Analytics LLC (“we”, “us”, “our”). This policy explains what information we collect from users of the TruckDesk mobile application and web service, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

Information We Collect

When you use TruckDesk, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account information — your email address or phone number, used for authentication via one-time passcode (OTP)
  • Business data you enter — load details (broker, origin, destination, rate, mileage), expenses (category, amount, description, receipts), compliance documents, and invoice records
  • Fuel and IFTA data — city, state, gallons purchased, and vendor information for fuel expenses, used for IFTA reporting
  • Device information — device type, operating system version, and app version, collected automatically for troubleshooting

How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your identity and secure your account
  • To store and display your loads, expenses, compliance records, and invoices
  • To generate reports including IFTA fuel tax summaries and profit/loss statements
  • To send you notifications about compliance document expirations or flagged expenses
  • To process messages sent to MilesBot via WhatsApp for automated data entry
  • To improve the app and fix technical issues

WhatsApp Integration (MilesBot)

If you use the MilesBot WhatsApp integration, messages you send to our business number are processed to extract load and expense data. Message content is parsed for data entry purposes and is not stored beyond what is saved as structured records in your account. WhatsApp message delivery is handled by Meta’s WhatsApp Business API, subject to WhatsApp’s own privacy policy.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Supabase, a cloud database platform with servers in the United States. We use row-level security policies to ensure each user can only access their own data. Authentication tokens are stored locally on your device and transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only in these limited circumstances:

  • Fleet owners — if you are a driver in a fleet, the fleet owner can view your loads, expenses, and compliance status through their owner dashboard
  • Service providers — we use Supabase (database hosting) and Meta WhatsApp Business API (messaging). These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy policies
  • Legal requirements — if required by law, court order, or government request

Data Retention

We retain your business data for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, we will delete your personal information and business records within 30 days, except where retention is required by law (e.g., tax records).

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Export your load, expense, and invoice data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information below.

Children’s Privacy

TruckDesk is designed for business use by adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18 years of age. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child, we will delete it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy in the app or sending a notification. The “Effective” date at the top reflects the latest revision.
