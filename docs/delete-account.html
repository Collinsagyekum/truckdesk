Delete Your Account

If you would like to delete your TruckDesk account and all associated data, follow the steps below.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to cagyekum26@gmail.com from the email address associated with your TruckDesk account.
  2. Use the subject line: TruckDesk Account Deletion Request
  3. Include your full name as it appears in the app.
  4. We will confirm your identity and process the deletion within 30 days.

What Data Is Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Your account profile (email, name)
  • All load records
  • All expense records and receipt images
  • Compliance documents
  • Invoice records
  • WhatsApp/MilesBot message history

Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Once processed, your data cannot be recovered.

Data That May Be Retained

Certain records may be retained for a limited period as required by law:

  • Financial transaction records may be kept for up to 7 years for tax compliance purposes
  • Records required by active legal proceedings or government requests
